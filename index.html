Sample wording
  1. I am at least 18 years old and will show a valid government-issued photo ID.
  2. I am not under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  3. I understand a tattoo is a permanent change to my skin, and that removal is costly and may not fully work.
  4. I understand colors and lines can look different on skin than on paper, and can change as the tattoo heals and ages.
  5. I have told my artist about any allergies, skin conditions, or medications that could affect healing.
  6. I understand infection is possible if I do not follow the aftercare instructions I am given.
  7. I approve the design, spelling, and placement before the tattoo begins.
  8. I release Reign Tattoo and its artists from claims that come from a tattoo I asked for and approved.
